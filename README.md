# Subnautica Map — subnauticamap.org

**[subnauticamap.org](https://subnauticamap.org)** is a reference site for Unknown Worlds’ underwater survival series: full-screen **interactive maps**, **guides**, and **mod listings** you can browse in one place.

---

## About the games

### Subnautica (2018)

After the starship **Aurora** crashes on ocean planet **4546B**, you survive by diving, scanning, crafting tools and vehicles (Seamoth, PRAWN, Cyclops), building seabases, and piecing together story from PDAs and the world itself. Progression is about **oxygen, depth, and biome knowledge**—learning where it is safe to go and when to turn back.

### Subnautica: Below Zero

A **polar-focused** chapter on the same planet with a new protagonist, **surface ice**, **temperature**, and vehicles such as the **Seatruck**. It keeps the scan-and-build loop while rewarding **route planning** between cold air and warmer underwater pockets.

### Subnautica 2

The next major **underwater survival** entry, with marketing emphasis on **exploration, base building, crafting**, and **optional small-squad co-op** alongside solo play. Because it evolves through **early access–style updates**, always treat patch notes and your store page as the source of truth for features and saves.

## Common questions

**Do I need to play the games in order?**  
Below Zero is its own campaign; playing **Subnautica** first adds context but is not strictly required. Subnautica 2 is a new world and ruleset—treat earlier titles as optional background.

**Is there official online co-op in Subnautica or Below Zero?**  
Retail **Subnautica** and **Below Zero** are built around single-player progression; online play is only through **community projects** (compatibility varies by patch). **Subnautica 2** advertises co-op as a product feature—check Unknown Worlds’ channels for how access works in your phase of early access.

**Will my saves survive every patch?**  
Survival games can invalidate saves after large world updates. Keep **manual backups** and read **official patch notes** before updating.

**Are the maps “official”?**  
The atlases on subnauticamap.org are **fan-made** browser maps built from curated data and tooling such as MapLibre; they are not a substitute for in-game discovery or first-party map products.

**Where are privacy, terms, and contact?**  
Legal and site-policy pages are linked from the site footer, for example [About](https://subnauticamap.org/about), [Contact](https://subnauticamap.org/contact), [Privacy](https://subnauticamap.org/privacy), [Terms](https://subnauticamap.org/terms), and [Copyright](https://subnauticamap.org/copyright).
